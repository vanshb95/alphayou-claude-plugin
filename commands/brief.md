Brief for: $ARGUMENTS

Follow the market-brief skill. The first argument is a comma-separated list of up to five tickers. The second, if present, is a comma-separated list of up to five Hyperliquid symbols such as BTC or xyz:NVDA. The last, if it is a number, is the window in hours (24 by default, 168 at most). If there is no ticker or asset, ask which ones.
