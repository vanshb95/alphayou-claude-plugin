---
description: Check a fund's latest 13F changes, or rank tracked funds on the public scoreboard
argument-hint: [FUND NAME] [window]
---

Fund check for: $ARGUMENTS

Follow the fund-tracker skill. If a fund name is given, show its latest 13F changes and its scoreboard row. If not, show the scoreboard for the window given, or the default window. A window is one of 4 years, 3 years, 2 years, 1 year or 6 months.
